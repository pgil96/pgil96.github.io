01About Us

Your trusted partner in commodities trading

Based in Spain, Horsan Brokers Company SL is an independent firm specializing in petroleum commodities trading. We work directly with producers and end-buyers to execute efficient and transparent transactions across global markets.

With over 15 years of experience, we bring deep market knowledge, an extensive network of counterparties, and a commitment to professional integrity in every deal we structure.

  • Crude oil & refined petroleum products
  • Direct access to producers and end-buyers
  • Full KYC compliance and due diligence
02What We Do

Our Services

We move petroleum commodities efficiently between counterparties across the globe.

Crude Oil Trading

Connecting crude oil suppliers with refineries and trading companies. We handle term contracts and spot trades across multiple grades and origins.

Refined Products

Gasoline, diesel, jet fuel, fuel oil, naphtha and more. We source and match refined product flows to meet your procurement or offtake needs.

International Trade

We originate deals, support contract negotiation, and oversee smooth cargo execution between counterparties across international markets.

Coal Trading

Expanding our offering with a coal trading line of Nigerian origin, connecting producers with international buyers across global markets.

05Compliance

Compliance & Due Diligence

We are committed to transparency and full regulatory compliance in every transaction we facilitate.

KYC / KYB

Full verification of every counterparty — identity, ownership and track record — before any transaction is initiated.

AML & Sanctions Screening

Screening against international sanctions lists — OFAC, EU and UN — with strict anti-money-laundering controls on every deal.

Traceability & Documentation

Certificates of origin, quality and quantity, supported by independent inspection agencies such as SGS or Intertek.

Regulatory Adherence

Operations conducted in accordance with the applicable laws and trade regulations of every jurisdiction we work in.
